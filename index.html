The Hebrew Bible as Music

A Twenty-Year Interdisciplinary Study

This site presents the tools extracted from my database that are the byproducts of a 20-year project begun in St Andrews, Scotland in 2006 and Oxford, England in 2010. My desire was first to learn Hebrew and, after Oxford, to rediscover the embedded music in the text.

You will find all the following items linked by volume in the volume directory Music Scores.

  • Music scores for each chapter (929 x .mscz files).
  • The .xml files that can be read and formatted by any music program.
  • Colour-coded pages that reveal shapes in the music. This concordance of contours in the text allows you to find where else in the Bible these musical shapes are used.
