Ornament Usage by Recitation Pitch

The first characteristic of the deciphering key to the musical sense of the accents is the separation of the accents below the text from those above. Those below the text define the recitation pitch. Those above are ornamentation relative to that defined pitch. The usage of ornaments by pitch gives some insight into the music of a particular section of the text. The table below shows the usage of ornaments by recitation pitch. We can see immediately which ornaments are rarely or frequently used and on which pitches.

The first two on the list are two ornaments on a single syllable. The first is unique in the Bible. It occurs in Genesis 35:22 on the word בִּבְכֹ֥ות (bar 221 in the image below). This happens also in Exodus 20, Deuteronomy 5 and Isaiah 23:8.
