The Hebrew Bible as Music

The Project

This site presents the tools extracted from my database that are the byproducts of a 20 year project begun in St Andrews, Scotland in 2006 and Oxford, England in 2010. My desire was first to learn Hebrew and after Oxford, to rediscover the embedded music in the text.

The tools are:

  • The music scores in raw xml form and finished one-line scores, one or many per verse in the directory Music Scores,
  • A spreadsheet of all the musical shapes that may help in-depth analysis,
  • early tests of python to allow analysis of sequences and motifs,
  • A JSON database of the trace generated from my creation of the music files.
  • Python files that analyse the complete Tanach and a suite of possible extensions.
  • and the matrix containing links to my concordance.

The music scores (929 x mscz) are line by line verses of each chapter. There are from one to four lines required for a verse. The xml files (1:1 with mscz files) are the same but with the fully available xml tree for analysis by scripts. These have been verified for completeness after being generated from my translation database.

Note that the xml files may be opened with most music software

Developed over twenty years, the project has led to a complete musical representation of the full text of the Hebrew Bible. Translation and music together are presented in the 18 volume series: The Music of the Bible. The first two volumes, The Book of Job, and the Psalms are available. The remaining volumes are complete and in the production lineup.
